# CALBOK / On Record

**Calls fade. Receipts stay.**

CALBOK is the verifiable memory behind market predictions. Its voice is direct, curious, and accountable. Use concrete verbs: lock, verify, remember, recall. Describe what the evidence shows. Never imply guaranteed accuracy, invented performance, or verified authorship from a pasted source.

## Identity

The mark combines an open C, a conversation, and a receipt's notched edge. The orange arrow carries a call forward into its record. Use the mark at 24px or larger, with at least one quarter of its width as clear space. Use the wordmark for first introductions and the symbol for compact navigation and favicons.

Space Grotesk is the display face; DM Sans handles reading; IBM Plex Mono is reserved for evidence, timestamps, and small labels. System sans-serif and monospace fallbacks keep the interface usable if web fonts are unavailable. Keep body copy readable; avoid setting paragraphs in uppercase.

## Visual language

Receipt cards use paper surfaces, dashed dividers, aligned fields, and restrained torn edges. Dark forest navigation anchors the lighter journal. Numbered circular diagrams show how a settled call informs the next one. Orange highlights actions and the original claim; outcome colors always come with a text label.

References supplied by the user informed receipt proportions, dashboard hierarchy, and flywheel structure. Reference images and their example statistics are not reproduced in the product. The hero receipt is explicitly an illustration. Real profiles and performance continue to derive only from stored receipts.
